Why it's built this way
  • Thursday is fixed to Miguel because it's Ale's office day — she's never on hook for drop-off/pick-up then.
  • Friday shifts to Ale by 5pm and Saturday goes fully to her because that's when Miguel's MBA classes land — it's a real trade, not a favor.
  • Sunday mornings are Miguel-solo on purpose, so Ale gets one guaranteed lie-in a week to offset the Saturdays.
  • The 8pm/8:45pm workout split alternates who goes first — track it loosely and swap if one of you is short on gym time that week.
